    <p class="product-kicker">Pageprint · Privacy</p>
    <h1>Your screenshots stay with you.</h1>
    <p class="document-intro">Pageprint creates screenshots locally in your browser and downloads them directly to your computer. The developer does not receive, store, or process captured pages.</p>

    <section class="document-section"><h2>What stays local</h2><ul><li>Captured page content and page URLs</li><li>Intermediate viewport images and the finished PNG</li><li>Your successful-capture count</li><li>Your cached Pageprint entitlement, after purchase</li></ul></section>
    <section class="document-section"><h2>What Pageprint does not do</h2><ul><li>Does not upload screenshots or captured page content.</li><li>Does not operate a capture cloud or Pageprint account system.</li><li>Does not collect analytics, telemetry, advertising data, or crash reports.</li><li>Does not automatically monitor browsing or capture pages in the background.</li><li>Does not execute remote code.</li></ul></section>
    <section class="document-section"><h2>When Pageprint runs</h2><p>Pageprint captures only after you deliberately click its toolbar icon or invoke its keyboard shortcut. Chrome grants temporary access to the current page for that action. Unsupported browser and extension pages remain unavailable.</p></section>
    <section class="document-section"><h2>Local storage</h2><p>Pageprint stores its successful-capture count in local browser storage. After a purchase, it also stores the license key, the license provider's installation identifier, and a provider-verified entitlement locally so ordinary captures can continue without an online license check.</p></section>
    <section class="document-section"><h2>Purchase and license verification</h2><p>The planned paid release uses Lemon Squeezy as merchant of record and license provider. Lemon Squeezy handles payment, card, customer, tax, receipt, and refund information under its policies; Pageprint never receives card details.</p><p>Activating, checking, or deactivating a license sends only the license key and installation identifier directly to Lemon Squeezy's license API. Page content, page URLs, screenshots, and browsing history are not included.</p></section>
    <section class="document-section"><h2>Removing data</h2><p>Removing Pageprint clears the data stored by the extension in your browser. Downloaded PNG files remain wherever you saved them and can be deleted like any other local file.</p></section>
    <section class="document-section"><h2>Website analytics are separate</h2><p>The Pageprint extension does not contain the analytics used on krwill.xyz. Website analytics does not receive Pageprint screenshots, page URLs, usage counts, or license data.</p></section>
    <section class="document-section"><h2>Contact</h2><p>Questions about Pageprint or this privacy statement can be sent to <a href="mailto:support@krwill.xyz">support@krwill.xyz</a>.</p><p>Effective August 15, 2026.</p></section>
